I am a Computer Science PhD student at Northwestern University (NU), advised by Prof. [Annie Liang](https://www.anniehliang.com/) and Prof. [Ben Golub](https://bengolub.net/).

My research lies at the intersection of computer science and economics. I study how computational models can capture, evaluate, and explain human reasoning and scientific innovation. Using tools from machine learning and data science, I analyze decision making, strategic interaction, and the evolution and transfer of models across domains.

Before Northwestern, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Chicago (UChicago), advised by Prof. Ian Foster and Prof. Kyle Chard. Before that, I hold a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).
